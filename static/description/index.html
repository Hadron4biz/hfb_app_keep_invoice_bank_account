Keep Invoice Bank Account

Pick a bank account on an invoice and it stays picked - Odoo stops swapping it back.

The problem

A partner can have several bank accounts on file, and sometimes an invoice needs a specific one - not the first in the list. Odoo recomputes the Recipient Bank field every time something it depends on changes, most often the partner itself, and that recompute always resets it to the partner's first account. The deliberate choice quietly disappears.

How it works

1

Pick a bank account

Set Recipient Bank on the invoice to the account you want.

2

Keep editing normally

Change the partner, save, reopen - anything else stays untouched.

3

It sticks

Your choice is never silently swapped back. Clear the field yourself and Odoo's normal default takes over again.

Where it applies

Any invoice, bill, credit note or receipt (account.move). Requires the Invoicing / Accounting app. No new models, no extra configuration, no security rules - install it and manually chosen bank accounts simply stop getting reset.
